Love’s Customer Segmentation Analytics

Built a Python-based customer analytics workflow connecting customer, campaign, product-sales, store, and historical data to compare engagement and promotional outcomes.

5CONNECTED DATASETS
20,000DRIVERS
29.99%MEDIUMDISCOUNT ACHIEVE RATE

THE WORK

Used Python and pandas to integrate five source tables using driver and store keys, checked key coverage and unmatched records, and engineered metrics for campaign participation, achievement, fuel activity, merchandise sales, and store visits.

WHAT THE ANALYSIS SHOWED

  • MediumDiscount led engagement: a 20.95% join rate and 29.99% achieve rate.
  • Campaign goals matter: DEF Extra showed the strongest incremental fuel-gallon and visit impact in the original comparison, while Merch Extra was strongest for merchandise-focused impact.
  • Behavior beat a demographic assumption: age had essentially no meaningful relationship with gallons per visit (Pearson r = −0.05).

BUSINESS IMPLICATION

Prioritize behavior-based targeting, test incentive levels, and select campaign objectives deliberately. Strong historical engagement is a reason to test a targeting strategy, rather than proof of future lift.

Scope & limitations

Portfolio scope: the repository reconstructs the analysis from the original case data and final write-up. Reconstruction limits are documented. Raw competition datasets are not distributed.
