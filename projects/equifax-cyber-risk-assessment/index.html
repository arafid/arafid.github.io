MY PRIMARY CONTRIBUTION

My main presentation responsibility was the Cyber Risk Landscape section. I explained the four 12-month planning ranges and the reasoning behind them, connecting outside-in evidence with Equifax-specific exposure, controls, business impact, assumptions, and uncertainty.

THE FOUR SCENARIOS

  • Sensitive data compromise or manipulation.
  • Critical decision-service disruption.
  • Trusted third-party or partner compromise.
  • AI-agent or machine-identity misuse.

RISK FORECAST MODEL / STRUCTURED UNCERTAINTY

The technical artifact here is a scenario-based forecasting structure: combine outside-in threat evidence with Equifax-specific exposure, existing controls, business impact, and assumptions to produce planning ranges rather than false precision.

01 / EvidenceExternal threat patterns
02 / ContextExposure, controls, dependencies
03 / Forecast12-month planning range
04 / ActionControl priority + roadmap
Sensitive data compromise5–15%

Data sensitivity and exploitation risk balanced against security maturity, detection, and cloud-native controls.

Decision-service disruption8–20%

Ransomware/disruption exposure balanced against cloud resilience, recovery, and failover practices.

Third-party compromise12–30%

Broad partner dependency surface with risk that cannot be fully controlled internally.

AI / machine-identity misuse10–25%

Growing automation exposure with guardrails, human oversight, and governance still maturing.

Forecasting lesson

Ranges are more honest than a single-point probability when evidence is uncertain. The value is in making assumptions explicit and connecting the forecast to concrete controls and owners.

These are team-developed academic planning estimates, not published Equifax incident rates or outputs from an internal Equifax risk model.

FROM FORECASTS TO PRIORITIES

The team’s 36-month roadmap prioritized Zero Trust and privileged access management, operational resilience, third-party oversight, and AI / machine-identity governance. The emphasis was on measurable control outcomes and clear executive accountability.

Scope & limitations

Academic, outside-in assessment using public information and course frameworks. Forecasts are team-developed planning estimates, not published Equifax incident rates. Potential control gaps are hypotheses, not findings from an internal audit.
