# La Cocina de la Abuela Mobile App Planning

Supporting portfolio project from **MIS 4433: Project Management** at the University of Oklahoma.

This team project used a fictionalized/local-business case to plan a mobile application for La Cocina de la Abuela Taco Truck. The work focused on the project-management side of a technology implementation rather than building a production application.

## Business problem

The case described a taco truck that relied on in-person and phone orders and had limited digital customer engagement. The proposed mobile solution was intended to support customer ordering, loyalty tracking, notifications, employee scheduling/time entry, and management visibility into sales and operations.

## What the team planned

The project was completed through three milestone submissions that together covered the project lifecycle:

1. **Scope, requirements, and schedule** — functional and non-functional requirements, project phases, dependencies, critical path, timeline, and initial mobile-app mockups.
2. **Cost, procurement, risk, and quality** — budget assumptions, vendor/resource planning, a ten-item risk register, quality targets, testing approaches, accessibility, payment security, and updated mockups.
3. **Stakeholders, communication, change, and closeout** — stakeholder analysis, a communication register, final screen concepts, change control, handoff/closeout planning, and lessons learned.

## Representative planning artifacts

- Five-phase schedule: planning, design, development, testing, and launch
- Critical-path sequence from requirements through deployment
- Cost and procurement planning for app-store accounts, payment processing, test devices, cloud hosting, and development/testing services
- Risk register covering scope creep, app-store rejection, third-party downtime, budget overruns, compatibility, data breach, staffing, adoption, testing delays, and compliance
- Quality targets including app load time, crash-free rate, usability, accessibility, and secure payment processing
- Stakeholder and communication plans for customers, employees, management, sponsors, developers, QA, and test users
- Change log and project closeout plan
- Mobile-app screen concepts for ordering, customization, checkout, loyalty, profile, promotions, employee scheduling, and management dashboards

## My documented learning

My individual reflection focused especially on stakeholder communication and procurement. I described the value of matching stakeholder groups to the right communication channels—for example, development updates, sponsor communication, and structured customer feedback—and monitoring whether those channels were effective. I also highlighted procurement concepts such as contract types and make-or-buy analysis.

## Portfolio scope

This is presented as an **academic team project and planning exercise**. The mobile app was not deployed as a production system. Budgets, schedule outcomes, risk scores, quality targets, change events, and closeout results in the original class deliverables were scenario/planning artifacts rather than audited real-world results.

The raw milestone documents, grading rubric, and individual reflection are intentionally not published here. Instead, the portfolio uses a cleaned case study and an artifact digest that preserve the meaningful work without class-only instructions or personal material.
