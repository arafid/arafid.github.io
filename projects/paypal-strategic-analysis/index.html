PayPal Strategic Analysis

A team capstone examining how PayPal could strengthen its competitive position as digital checkout becomes more crowded and consumer payment behavior changes.

SWOTMARKET CONTEXT
5 ForcesCOMPETITIVE PRESSURES
VRINCAPABILITY ASSESSMENT

THE QUESTION

How can a payments platform turn its scale, data, customer relationships, and merchant network into a more defensible advantage? The analysis connected external competitive pressure with internal resources and execution challenges.

THE TEAM’S RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Strengthen internal innovation: direct more attention toward R&D and integration of existing capabilities.
  • Expand in-person transactions: evaluate a broader merchant-platform role connecting online and physical commerce.
  • Test the risks: account for entrenched POS competitors, technological disruption, and BNPL regulatory complexity.

THE TAKEAWAY

Transaction history and the data behind fraud detection can be more defensible than an AI model alone. Sustainable advantage also depends on integration, execution, and the ability to adapt.

PROJECT CONTEXT

Academic team capstone for BAD 4013: Business Strategy and Policy at the University of Oklahoma, Spring 2026. The team included Alera Netzel, Dominique Cootes, Tenley Hyde, Areebah Khan, Caden Reed, Jackson Craig, and Afnan Rafid.

Scope & limitations

Historical academic analysis based on publicly available information and course strategy frameworks. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by PayPal.
